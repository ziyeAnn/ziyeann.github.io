## Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of artificial intelligence, educational technology, and human-AI interaction.

- **Artificial Intelligence in Education:** Large language models and intelligent agents for teaching and learning.
- **Human-AI Interaction:** Human-centered collaboration between learners, educators, and AI systems.
- **Intelligent Learning Systems:** Adaptive learning environments, learner modeling, and pedagogical planning.
- **Educational Technology:** Integrating emerging technologies into practical educational applications.

## Selected Research

**Controllable Programming Tutoring with Belief-Based Pedagogical Planning**

My current research focuses on controllable LLM-based programming tutoring systems. This work explores belief-based learner modeling, pedagogical decision-making, and teacher-guided constraints to support adaptive and personalized programming instruction.
