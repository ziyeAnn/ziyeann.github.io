I am a Lecturer in the Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering at Chongqing College of Mobile Communication. I received my Master's degree from the University of Sydney, Australia, with an academic background in Information Technology and Software Engineering. I also hold a Bachelor's degree in Internet of Things Engineering from Chongqing University of Education.

My research interests include Artificial Intelligence in Education, Educational Technology, Human-AI Interaction, and Intelligent Learning Systems. I am particularly interested in exploring how large language models and intelligent agents can support personalized learning, adaptive instruction, and educational innovation. My work focuses on the intersection of artificial intelligence, learning technologies, and practical educational applications.

## Research Interests

- **Artificial Intelligence in Education:** Exploring the application of large language models and intelligent agents in teaching and learning.
- **Human-AI Interaction:** Investigating effective collaboration between humans and AI systems in educational contexts.
- **Intelligent Learning Systems:** Developing adaptive and personalized learning environments through learner modeling and pedagogical planning.
- **Educational Technology:** Integrating emerging technologies into practical teaching and interdisciplinary educational applications.

## Current Research

**Controllable Programming Tutoring with Belief-Based Pedagogical Planning**

My current research focuses on designing controllable LLM-based programming tutoring systems. The work explores belief-based learner modeling, pedagogical decision-making, and teacher-guided constraints to support adaptive and personalized programming instruction.
