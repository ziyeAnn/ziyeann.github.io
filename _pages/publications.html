--- layout: archive title: "Publications" permalink: /publications/ author_profile: true --- ## Research My research interests include Artificial Intelligence in Education, Educational Technology, Human-AI Interaction, and Intelligent Learning Systems. My work focuses on exploring how large language models and intelligent agents can support personalized learning, adaptive instruction, and educational innovation.
